Introduction

Many good things happened these last two weeks -- which were so busy I couldn't get out last week's newsletter.

First is the King's Academy Covid-19 Symposium. The resources generated by it are meant to benefit students and teachers everywhere. Explore the Symposium website for short lessons and discussions that may be useful in your schools for providing an interdisciplinary understanding of this moment in history.

The second is the Athena Summer Fellowship. The Athena platform is getting a refresh this year, including some additional tools for teacher collaboration. Apply for a summer fellowship or share the opportunity with colleagues. It's an extraordinary opportunity to develop your curriculum within a flexible structure.

And of course, we're back into the swing of articles. Look out this week for writing on leadership in times of crisis -- and some good, old fashioned pedagogy, too.

Enjoy!

Peter Nilsson King's Academy Jordan
