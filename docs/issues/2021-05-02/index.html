Introduction

An excellent week.

Nuanced writing (or speaking) about nuanced topics in both of the featured articles this week. The lead piece on the difference between DEI training and education offers a look into what makes for truly effective learning in the DEI space, which the article posts is richer than training.

The second feature post is a TED talk by Adam Grant. The last six minutes are the most powerful. He presents (throughout) a case that humility and acknowledging our own limits -- especially as leaders -- is what creates space for the best leadership.

Elsewhere: the posts on rubrics in the assessment category are excellent, the tributes to Shakespeare (his birthday recently passed!) are thoughtful and sensational, the pieces on pedagogy are practical, and Austin Kleon's reflection on the difference between languishing and being dormant is an optimistic take on what we're all feeling right now. I agree with him.

Enjoy these, and others!

Peter Nilsson
