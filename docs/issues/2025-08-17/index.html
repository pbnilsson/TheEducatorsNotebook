Introduction

A favor...

An excellent issue —

But first, a favor to ask! I have two projects in SXSW EDU’s panel picker, and I would be grateful if you would check them out and give them a “heart”. What are they about? Well, I’m happy to share two BIG projects that are coming to fruition in the next year: first, coauthor Maya Bialik and I have a book coming out this winter on AI and Education. Working title and information are below. Second, the one-act musical I wrote six years ago and performed with 25 colleagues at a school meeting is now growing into a full length show — and it, too, is in SXSW EDU’s panel picker for next spring. See below for descriptions of both, and please consider giving them some love by clicking on the heart to increase the likelihood that they’ll be live this coming March at SXSW EDU. Thank you!

NEW BOOK:

* “Irreplaceable: How AI Changes Everything (and Nothing) About Teaching & Learning” + While AI’s accelerating growth enables (and demands) some pedagogical reinvention, much of the practice of teaching & learning remains grounding in time-tested principles from learning science. This session stakes out where and how learning happens — teachers preparing, students learning, and both together in class — dives into learning science for each setting, and introduces usable insights for teachers to deepen their practice. With perspective-bending insights, this session reframes both the creative work of teaching and the way we see AI, both philosophically and practically. + (Click here to see author interviews, and give this session some support.)

NEW MUSICAL:

* Schooled: The Musical + Get an early look at a show in development that started with a performance by 25 teachers at a school meeting. Now, it is growing into a full length show. In this Hamilton-meets-The Sound of Music-meets-Good Night and Good Luck story, discover the lives of three high school students, and how they and their teachers learn about who they are, who they dream to be, and what they can and cannot learn in school. It’s a joy-filled, coming-of-age story set against the backdrop of today’s culture wars. Blending contemporary and traditional styles – and even good pedagogy – the show will follow with a talk back with the show’s creator and a moderator. + (Click here to see an excerpt of the piece that launched the show, and give this performance some support.)

Thank you for your consideration!

“The Wellness and Oversight for Psychological Resources Act prohibits anyone from using AI to provide mental health and therapeutic decision-making, while allowing the use of AI for administrative and supplementary support services for licensed behavioral health professionals. This will also protect vulnerable children amid the rising concerns over AI chatbot use in youth mental health services.”

On Sycophancy And Adolescence: “AI Always Agrees With Your Kids, And That’s A Problem”

Psychology Today · 08/08/25

“AI systems learn to be agreeable because engagement is their primary function. Think of it like having a friend who always says, "Yes, you're right" to avoid conflict. The AI learns to mirror your opinions and validate your feelings because that keeps you using the platform. It's how these systems are programmed to work. When children encounter any type of struggle—social, academic, or even which jeans to buy—they increasingly turn to AI as their first step in problem-solving. The AI responds by reframing their thoughts as confirmation of what they already think. This positively reinforces their ideas even when those ideas might be objectively flawed.”
