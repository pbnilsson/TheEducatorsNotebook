Educator’s Notebook #45 (September 7, 2014)

Several articles on what makes great teachers, several articles on what's overhyped in education these days, and some astonishing technological developments.

Also worth perusing (in the original sense: reading carefully) is the New York Times article on Bill Gates and the development of the Big History curriculum. The article describes how the course came to be and grow--an entrepreneurial and organic process--and offers a notable contrast to the top down, big budget Gates Foundation approach to the common core.

And, an ever growing body of research about the benefits of music for learning.

Enjoy!

FEATURED

Is Verbal Ability the Greatest Indicator of Teacher Success?

Forbes · 08/31/14

“Several decades after the Equality of Education Opportunity Study (a.k.a. the Coleman Study) revealed teacher verbal competence to be a strong predictor of student outcomes, an Abell Foundation study on teacher certification found that ‘the teacher attribute found consistently to be most related to raising student achievement is verbal ability.’”
