Introduction

A truly excellent week.

The two feature posts offer meaningful research and reflection on two crucial topics: teacher professional development and student engagement. They are both valuable for schools exploring these areas.

What does it mean to "decolonize the curriculum"? The CIS piece under curriculum meaningfully explores the overly-loaded phrase. It examines the current historical moment in the context of postcolonialism and postmodernism, and then explores the phrase "decolonize the curriculum" not in its polarizing misrepresentation, but in a way examines the objectives and opportunities of curriculum in a diverse world. It's a worthy read.

I'm also partial to the STEM piece on why Abraham Lincoln loved Euclid and geometry. It echoes this playful rap about geometry and math class I wrote for the musical.

Lastly, King's Academy's 12th Commencement will livestream at 10:30am ET. Visitors are welcome at this link .

Enjoy this week's terrific selection!
