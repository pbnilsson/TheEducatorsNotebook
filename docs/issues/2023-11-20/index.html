Introduction

Happy Thanksgiving week to American readers!

Our feature articles this week start with a tool for custom college lists that you can create with an interactive database smartly made by The New York Times. Also find a tactical take on how to have productive disagreements on sensitive topics, as guided by two leaders from the NYU School of Law.

Elsewhere in this issue, find three interesting pieces on apprenticeships and work-based learning in the Curriculum and Other sections. Also, November is Native American Heritage Month — find an excellent resource in Diversity/Inclusion with approaches to integrating resources from Native American history in your classes. Last, see two compelling pieces in the STEM section on math pedagogy: one on how we conceive of math instruction at a high level, and another on effective components of math games.

In this week’s A.I. Update, check out how the arrival of A.I. is renewing the importance of the Humanities. Also, find a few helpful takes on the Sam Altman/OpenAI whirlwind weekend.

These and more, enjoy!

Peter
