Introduction

On the benefits of singing together, on measuring creativity, on social media practices, and more.

Also, two of the three feature articles this week are on the aftermath of the shooting in Parkland, Florida. This particular event has remained in the headlines for longer than most, and the student voice has been so loud and clear, it gives hope that change in regards to school safety (and general safety) may actually occur.

Lastly, are you interested in influencing the future of math instruction? Check out the free conference in Geneva that aims to bring stakeholders together to discuss international math curricula.

Enjoy!

Peter
