Introduction

Lots of writing this week on the start of school.

Labor Day Weekend is here in the States, and classes for many schools are starting up this week. Check out Athena for how hundreds of educators choose to start their classes, building relationships with students, introducing foundational principles of their disciplines, and more. Accounts are free.

The feature articles this week touch on familiar themes of teacher mentorship and fostering civil discussion on hot topics. These are adding to a growing body of writing on these topics that I aim to consolidate in the time ahead.

Elsewhere this week, find a variety of pieces that revive some topics that are less in the spotlight than they once were: a piece on gamification, a state of MOOCs, a call for badging instead of grades, and a host of interesting pieces in the "other" category at the end.

As ever, there's much afoot in the world today. Enjoy!

FEATURED

Interdependent Mentoring Helps Both Mentor And Mentee

Edutopia · 08/26/22

“A difference between traditional and interdependent mentoring is that traditional mentoring can be one-sided. New teachers may feel compelled to show deference to experienced veterans, and mentors may be asked to respond to problems they’re not comfortable solving without additional help. In contrast, interdependent mentoring partnerships prioritize both teachers’ contributions. Mentors are appreciated for their deep knowledge and wisdom, and new teachers are valued for their innovative ideas and fresh instructional insights.”
