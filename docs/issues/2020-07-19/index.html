Introduction

It's been about a month since the last newsletter, an uncharacteristic gap brought about by the extraordinary demands of this time. But I hope to be back into the routine in the coming weeks, with some additional offerings and opportunities coming soon.

Until then, this week's newsletter captures a selection of posts from the past month. Most notable among them is Jennifer Gonzalez's summary of differences between online and face-to-face teaching. Also find several articles from Global Online Academy that may be particularly useful for your planning for the fall term.

Enjoy these and others this week, and looking forward to getting back into the swing of things, whether where you are will be online or in person.

Peter Nilsson King's Academy Jordan
