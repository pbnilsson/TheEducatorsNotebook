Introduction

An extraordinary week of articles and materials.

The two featured pieces are vastly different, but both meaningful.

The research by Coney Island Prep is the most thorough research I've seen: 130 slides of global analysis, research synthesis, information visualization and more. It's an extraordinary resource for anyone still making decisions about the fall. and it's very accessible.

The 3-minute short film "Numb" offers a remarkably emotionally resonant depiction of what it means for a teen to live in isolation. Impressive work by a promising and inventive young artist.

Also explore: the Boston area administrators who decided to try for themselves what they were proposing for the students -- so they rode the bus in marked seats, sat in rearranged classrooms, etc. We should all be doing this.

And: conversations about cancel culture continue to evolve. One op-ed in the New York Times meaningfully summarizes various angles on this current moment, through a collection of reflections on the recent open letter published in Harper's.

Enjoy these, and more!

Peter Nilsson King's Academy Jordan
