Introduction

What a year it has been. I'll be taking the month of July off from sharing articles, but will recommence in August. In the meantime, in this final newsletter for the school year, here are some greats from the past few weeks:

The feature article on rigor asks good questions. For me, rigor, based on its etymological definition, has meant setting clear and high expectations and holding students accountable for (and supporting them meeting) those expectations. A rigorous class or school is inflexible about expecting (and supporting) high quality work. This doesn't have to do with workload or stress or strictness, but about setting up students to be able to accomplish what we want them to accomplish.

For the other feature article: we all know that a key part of what we are teaching our students is character -- we seek to develop not only content mastery and skills mastery, but character traits that lead to happy, healthy, and successful lives. But how do we teach these in the context of our academic disciplines? The feature article by the Center for Curriculum Design traces character development back to different disciplines. Which subjects are optimal for teaching creativity, or resilience, or leadership? Explore the second feature article for ideas on these questions.

These and more... enjoy!

(And see you in August.)
