Introduction

I was a little stunned at first by the tone of the feature article on chalkboards. If we are surprised that the chalkboard is an effective tool, then we have bought into the myth that gadgets are necessary for good teaching. It's how we use tools and technology--not the fact of them--that makes the difference. This article is a good reminder that even writing was once a technology, and that great teaching and great learning come in many forms.

Also, the other feature: I love the irony of light existentialist humor.

Lastly, Elon Musk's twenty minute keynote might highlight his awkward speech patterns, but it paints a compelling vision of the future.

Enjoy!

Pete

FEATURED

Researcher Goes To Japan, Finds Chalkboards, Is Shocked

Larry Cuban · 04/26/15

"The focus in Japanese education is not on how many innovations they rush to implement or how many new gadgets students get to use. Instead, educators focus on collecting evidence of effectiveness and leveraging technology resources (whether it’s a chalkboard or a smartboard) with purpose and intentionality to enhance and facilitate teaching and learning opportunities. As stated in a recent Japanese publication from the Future Schools Project, ‘Traditional education will be valued while those parts of it that should be extended, broadened, or deepened will evolve significantly.’”
