Introduction

Excellent posts, both Covid-19 related and pedagogical.

Also, this week: applications are open for the 2020 Athena summer fellowships. It's an ideal opportunity for teachers to develop and improve curriculum collaboratively and remotely. Grant-funded spots are limited, but Athena is expanding staffing this summer for school-sponsored teams of three or more teachers working on curriculum development and seeking feedback from a growing peer community. Read more about the possibilities at the link below.

Meanwhile, the news keeps coming. The two feature articles speak directly to this current crisis in both human and pedagogical ways. They are good reminders of the tactile, experiential world around us during our new, online experiences.

Also in the "Other" section are a series of videos that clearly illustrate the health benefits of handwashing, coughing into our sleeves, and not touching our faces. I hope they are useful where you are.

Best of luck in these unusual times.

Peter Nilsson King's Academy Jordan
